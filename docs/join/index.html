Join a Mercenaries 2 co-op game

A friend has invited you to play with them in the beta, a fan rebuild of Mercenaries 2: World in Flames.

  1. You need your own copy of Mercenaries 2: World in Flames (PC) installed. The beta plays it from your install; it brings no game files of its own.
  3. Unzip it anywhere and run m2-beta.exe. It finds the invite you copied and joins your friend.

If it doesn't join (say the copy was refused), run m2-beta.exe --join followed by this, from a command prompt in its folder:

Invite copied.

This link has no invite in it. Ask your friend to send the whole link their game gave them.

Windows only. The beta is open source; only its own code is in the download. It connects straight to your friend's game, or through a relay server when your routers don't allow that; nothing about your game is kept anywhere. Source
