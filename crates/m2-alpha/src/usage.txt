m2-alpha: Mercenaries 2 on a native Rust engine, from your own install.

m2-alpha [options]
  (no options, or only --install/--config/--size/--vsync/--fullscreen/
  --no-boot/--smarter-ai/--dev/--skip-mode-on: the whole game, as --alpha)
  --alpha                the game as it launches: the boot movies (EA,
                         Pandemic), the front end (Enter starts), the first
                         contract with its movies, then on to free roam.
                         Movies need ffmpeg (M2_FFMPEG, beside m2-alpha, or
                         on the PATH); Enter/Space/Escape skips one
  --no-boot              with --alpha: no boot movies or front end
  --dev                  with --alpha: the developer's keys too (F4 free
                         camera, F5/F9 quick save and load, F3 culling, N
                         gait), which the whole game's launch leaves out;
                         the other launches (--mission, --spawn, --model,
                         --fly...) always have them
  --skip-mode-on         with --alpha (or --mission --movies
                         --screenshot): the scripts' developer skip mode
                         (on so their intro movies end at once) stays on
                         all session, instead of ending once their start
                         has run (the default; --skip-mode-off is still
                         taken). Left on, the flow skips its fades and the
                         move into the PMC HQ
  --movies               with --mission --screenshot: the scripts' movies
                         wait; each is decoded offscreen and pictured
                         (<out>-movie-<name>.ppm)
  --front-end            with --screenshot: the front end's own menus,
                         pictured (<out>-front-<n>.ppm) once up and after
                         each --shell-key
  --shell-key <code>     a Flash key code pressed in --front-end (13 Enter,
                         37/39 left/right, 38/40 up/down, 27 Escape;
                         0 presses nothing)
  --after-contract <s>   with --mission --autoplay --screenshot: play on
                         this long after the contract is done, logging
                         where she is (Enter pressed every 4 s)
  --install <dir>        the game's install folder (holding data\vz.wad); the EA app
                         folder in Program Files is found without it
  --hero <name>          who to play: mattias, jennifer (default) or chris
                         (also `hero` in the settings, and the pause menu)
  --spawn <x>,<z>        where on the island to start (default: the PMC's
                         headquarters, where free roam begins after the
                         first contract; with --mission, 450,-1950)
  --fly                  start with the free camera instead of on foot
  --model <hash>         a model to show (hex name hash; repeatable)
  --clip <hash>[@<s>]    with --model: a clip for each model to play (hex
                         hash; repeatable, in model order); with @seconds,
                         held still that far in
  --turn <degrees>       with --model: turn the models (90 shows a side)
  --gait <gait>          idle, walk, run or sprint: what characters shown
                         with --model play first; with --screenshot on the
                         island, how the player moves before the picture
  --walk-seconds <s>     with --gait on the island: she goes on this long
                         (default 3), logging each second where she is, how
                         deep the sea is over her feet and whether she swims
                         (walk lines)
  --config <file>        settings file (default m2-alpha.toml)
  --size <w>x<h>         window size
  --vsync on|off
  --fullscreen           start fullscreen (at the mode the pause menu kept)
  --sea <metres>         sea level (default -36, the game's water shader)
  --double-sided         start with back-face culling off (as F3)
  --screenshot <out.ppm> render one frame offscreen and exit
  --drive                with --screenshot on the island: get in the
                         nearest parked vehicle and drive it forward first
                         (backing out first if it is boxed in, steering
                         round what it meets)
  --jump                 with --screenshot on the island: jump just before
                         the picture
  --throw                with --screenshot on the island: throw a grenade
                         just before the picture
  --bash                 with --screenshot on the island: a melee bash just
                         before the picture
  --reload               with --screenshot on foot: change the rifle's clip
                         --lead seconds before the picture
  --bash-clip <hash>     with --bash: bash with this clip (one of those the
                         "player meleebash clips:" line lists)
  --lead <s>             with --jump, --throw or --bash: seconds before the
                         picture she starts (default 0.2)
  --fight                with --screenshot on the island: shoot the nearest
                         soldier, rocket a vehicle, throw a grenade first,
                         then look at the body
  --traffic <s>          with --screenshot on the island: play this many
                         seconds before the picture, logging the traffic
                         spawned from the layers' spawners every 5 s (each
                         vehicle's place, speed and waypoint) and the five
                         spawners nearest her
  --fight-wait <s>       with --fight or --blast: wait this long before
                         the picture (with --blast, counted from the
                         rocket's blast)
  --blast                with --screenshot on the island: fire a rocket at
                         the fuel tank or barrel nearest where she starts
  --demolish <n>         with --screenshot on foot: rocket the nearest
                         building (health and a collapse in its template)
                         until it comes down, at most n rockets, logging
                         its damage; pictures -before, -1s, -2s and -6s
  --plough <kind>        with --drive: park facing the nearest fence,
                         plant, lamp or box (within 150 m) and drive
                         straight through it
  --vehicle <name>       with --drive: get in the nearest vehicle whose
                         name holds this (stryker, m1a1, amx30, hmmwv...)
  --look <degrees>       with --drive: stop and swing the camera this far
                         before the picture (turrets follow it)
  --riders               with --screenshot on the island: seat the riders of
                         the vehicles near her before the picture, and log
                         how many were drawn
  --take                 with --riders: she then takes the vehicle of the
                         nearest seated driver, who is put out by its door,
                         and logs where they went
  --spawns <n>           with --screenshot: list the n spawns nearest where
                         she starts (name, place, who they spawn)
  --face <degrees>       with --screenshot on foot: face this way first
                         (0 is +z, 90 is +x)
  --aim                  with --screenshot on foot: weapon up for the picture
  --hit-from <deg>[,..]  with --screenshot on foot: hits from these directions
                         (degrees clockwise from where the camera looks, 0
                         ahead) just before the picture, to see the hit arcs
  --hud-seconds <s>      with --screenshot: run the game's HUD this long before
                         the picture (default 2)
  --loading-screen <s>   with --screenshot: the picture is of the loading screen
                         this long after it came up
  --weapon rifle|rpg|gl  with --screenshot on foot: the weapon she has up
                         (gl: the supply crate's grenade launcher)
  --switch               with --screenshot on foot: switch weapons --lead
                         seconds before the picture (stow, then take up);
                         with --drive --fire: press the weapon switch (to
                         the machine gun) before firing
  --pickup               with --screenshot on foot: pick a weapon up --lead
                         seconds before the picture
  --satellite <stage>    with --screenshot on foot: call a Satellite Guided
                         Bomb on the spot 45 m ahead of her (the support
                         key, the use key, the view steered over it and
                         confirmed, the wheel pressed in each wedge); the
                         picture through the satellite's view (view), with
                         the timing wheel after the first press (wheel),
                         or --fight-wait seconds (at least 0.8) after the
                         bomb lands (bomb)
  --gates                with --screenshot: bring down each of the mission's
                         gates as the satellite bomb does, four times
                         (its pieces thrown as fast as in play, 0.6 and
                         1.6 times as fast, then as in play with the other
                         models mostly in its box and the island's statics
                         in it taken down), logging how its pieces set
                         off and where they are 2 s and 8 s on; first it
                         lists the other models and the statics in each
                         gate's box and pictures the place without it
                         (-gate1-without.ppm); pictures from 50 m south of
                         each, the first time, of the rest of it alone and
                         its pieces alone as it breaks (-gate1-rest.ppm,
                         -gate1-pieces.ppm), and 2 s and 8 s on
                         (-gate1-2s.ppm, -gate1-8s.ppm), with the pieces
                         that hardly moved and what they touch at 2 s; the
                         last time the same at 2 s and 8 s
                         (-gate1-alone-2s.ppm, -gate1-alone-8s.ppm);
                         before all that it reads the fight's sounds as
                         play does and logs which it found
  --closeup              with --screenshot on foot: the picture from close by
                         her right side, to see her hands
  --pda                  with --screenshot: the picture with her PDA open
  --binoculars <zoom>    with --screenshot: the picture through her
                         binoculars at zoom 1..7, their screen up
  --pause                with --screenshot: the picture with the game's
                         pause menu open, --pda-key's keys typed into it
                         one by one (a third of a second apart)
  --behind               with --screenshot on foot: the picture from close
                         behind her, to see the weapons on her back
  --slung rpg|gl         with --screenshot on foot: give her this weapon in
                         the launcher's place, on her back with the rifle up
  --probe                with --screenshot: list what rays from where she
                         stands hit (unseen walls, steps in the ground)
  --wreck                with --drive: wreck the vehicle at speed at the
                         end and log how it comes to rest
  --fire                 with --aim or --drive: fire just before the
                         picture
  --grip attach|hands    draw weapons on the hand's attach node as posed
                         (attach, the default), or held as worked out from
                         the hands (hands)
  --mission              run the game's mission scripts: the first contract
                         starts as in the game, with its objectives and
                         markers on screen (log: m2-alpha-mission.log);
                         with --screenshot, plays ten seconds of it first
  --mission-driver <lua> Lua to run before the scripts start, to test them
                         (crates/m2-game/examples/drivers/vzacon001.lua
                         plays the first contract); repeatable
  --hijack-crew <name>   every hijackable vehicle without a driver is
                         driven by this human template, to test hijacking
                         (I/K/J/L are the pad's Y/A/X/B; the movement
                         keys its stick)
  --hijack               with --screenshot --drive: hijack the vehicle
                         (--vehicle's) instead, answering every prompt
  --placed-check         with --mission: every 5 s, try each pickup, crate and
                         emplaced gun the layers have put out (logged as
                         placed-check lines), for testing
  --context <text>       with --mission: once the scripts offer a context
                         action whose prompt reads <text> (exit, talk; a
                         comma-separated list for each in turn), put her at
                         it and take it with the use key, logging where she
                         is after (context-check lines)
  --dialog-keys <keys>   with --mission --after-contract: the keys pressed in a
                         dialog box the scripts put up (up, down, accept,
                         back; comma-separated), the first 3 s after it
                         opens and each 2 s on (dialog-check lines)
  --medevac <s>          with --mission --after-contract: this long after the
                         contract, the pause menu's Medevac as confirmed, if
                         the scripts allow it then (medevac-check lines)
  --visit-faction <f>    with --mission --after-contract: 30 s after the
                         contract, put her by a soldier of faction <f> (All,
                         Chi, Gur, Oil, Pir, Vza), hurt one 15 s on, and log
                         who fights her (faction-check lines)
  --jump-with-enter      with --mission --autoplay --after-contract: the
                         Enter pressed after the contract jumps too, as the
                         pad's A; where she is while the scripts hold her
                         controls is logged (held lines)
  --props-check <hit>    with --mission: once she is ashore (and inside
                         --props-area x0,z0,x1,z1 if given), hit every placed
                         prop there by <hit> (vehicle, gun or blast), log what
                         each did against what the data says (props-check
                         lines and a summary), and stop; for testing
  --support <key>        with --mission: stock support item <key> (clusterbomb,
                         Support) and call it in once the scripts run, to
                         test their strikes and drops; repeatable
  --shop-check <keys>    with --mission --autoplay: once she is ashore, buy
                         every support item (all, or the comma-separated
                         keys) in a shop with cash given her, call each in
                         from the support menu, aim its designator, and log
                         what came of it (shop-check lines and a summary)
  --cheat-menu <page>    with --screenshot: the F7 cheat menu up at that page
                         (weapons, vehicles, support, player), alone over
                         black
  --cheat-check          with --mission --autoplay: once she is ashore, try
                         the F7 cheat menu's cheats (every weapon, a few
                         vehicles of each kind, every support item, a
                         strike, money, god mode, infinite ammo, a
                         teleport) and log what came of it (cheat-check
                         lines)
  --seated               with --screenshot --drive: load the seats' clips
                         first and show her sitting in the vehicle
                         (also <stem>-seated.ppm before it moves)
  --mission-seconds <s>  with --screenshot: play this long instead of ten
  --look-pickup <m>      with --screenshot: take the picture from this far
                         off the pickup lying nearest her, looking at it
  --mission-look <m>     with --mission --screenshot: take the picture from
                         this far from the nearest marker, looking at it
  --old-hud              our own stand-in HUD (health bar, weapon line,
                         crosshair) instead of the game's own
  --old-fx               fire and smoke as the old coloured spheres, not the
                         game's own particle effects (to compare)
  --no-fx                no fire or smoke drawn at all (to measure what
                         they cover)
  --smarter-ai           the enhanced, smarter enemy soldiers (not the
                         original game's; settings [enhanced.smarter_ai])
  --autoplay             with --mission --screenshot: she plays the contract
                         herself by its markers (runs to places, shoots the
                         marked soldiers, opens and shuts her PDA when the
                         scripts wait for it, calls a satellite bomb on the
                         marked gates, waits for the vehicles brought and
                         takes them, going a
                         way planned round walls, the sea and steep
                         ground), logging how it goes, until it is done or
                         --mission-seconds are up; each delivery is
                         pictured beside the screenshot as its helicopter
                         hovers (<out>-delivery1.ppm, ...) and once its
                         load is down (-delivery1-down.ppm), the start's
                         boat five seconds after she is seated in it
                         (-boat.ppm), her three seconds after she gets out
                         of it (-ashore.ppm), what each crate leaves
                         (-pickup1.ppm, ...), her PDA as she opens it
                         (-pda1.ppm, ...), the satellite's view as each
                         timing wheel comes up (-satellite1.ppm, ...) and
                         her view two seconds after each bomb (-bomb1.ppm)
  --write-config         print the settings file and exit

The keys and mouse are the PC game's defaults (its Controls page; each
can be rebound in the settings' [keys], with "MouseLeft", "MouseRight",
"MouseMiddle", "WheelUp" and "WheelDown" for the mouse):
On foot: WASD runs, Left Shift sprints, hold Left Ctrl to walk, Space
jumps, hold C to crouch and aim (B too, for now), I/J/K/L look.
E gets in or out of a vehicle (a row is parked ahead of where you start).
Driving: W/S throttle and reverse, A/D steer, Space brakes, R rights it.
Fighting (mouse captured): left button fires, Tab or the wheel switches
weapon, R reloads, right button or G throws a grenade, V picks the next
explosive, F or the middle button bashes (a supply crate breaks open),
X sets off C4, Y moves to the next seat. 4 or Right (the view switch) does
nothing yet.
Support: 1 or Up (and 2 or Down) opens the support menu and moves its
line, and E takes up the item's designator and looks through the
satellite: WASD moves its view, fire or E confirms, and fire or E as the
timing wheel's needle passes through each grey wedge sends the bomb (a
press outside them undoes the presses made; moving the view away puts
the wheel away). Tab or the wheel puts the menu away; at the view, 1 or 2
calls it off; so does being hurt. F1 opens and shuts the PDA, whose map
WASD or the arrows move (the first contract's scripts give the bomb once
she has looked at it).
Shop and money: H opens the shop (a tab for each faction's; what each
sells unlocks with contracts, or all at once with the settings'
[enhanced.shop] unlock_all = true). What she buys is in the support
menu: 1/2, the arrows or W/S pick the item, E calls it in. G throws its smoke,
flare or beacon; a laser designator is held on the spot with fire; a
satellite one is moved like the bomb's view and confirmed with fire.
Cash and fuel lying about are taken by walking over them. The game saves
itself after a contract (m2-alpha-autosave.toml, with her cash, stock and
the scripts' own data); putting the scripts back from it on loading is
not done yet.
The contract done, Enter closes its fanfare and the flow goes on.
M music on/off, a click captures the mouse (F2 lets it go), F11 or
Alt+Enter fullscreen,
Escape opens the game's own pause menu (its movie, once the HUD's scripts
run; up/down through it, left/right a setting, Enter): resume, save,
options (game, video, audio, controls) and quit. Video's Resolution and
Refresh Rate step through the monitor's modes; a rate is fullscreen at
that mode, Windowed a window of that size. Its kept settings go to the
settings file. The alpha's own settings (hero, field of view, mouse,
vsync, quit to desktop) fold in the top right. Escape closes it.
An Xbox pad (One, Series or 360) plays as the game's own layout has it:
left stick move (L3 sprint), right stick look, A jump, B melee, X reload,
Y get in/use, RT fire, LT crouch, LB throw, RB switch weapon, R3 aim,
d-pad up/down support, left next explosive, right detonate, Back PDA,
Start menu. Driving: A forward, X reverse, LT handbrake, Y out. In the
menus A is Enter, B back, the d-pad moves. While the pad is used, the
HUD's prompts show its buttons.
The developer's keys (--dev, or any launch but the whole game's): F4
switches to the free camera (WASD moves, Space/C up/down, Shift faster;
not while the scripts have her controls off) and back to her where she
was left, N next gait (--model), F3 culling, F5 quick save, F9 quick load
(the world only: the mission's scripts carry on as they were).

M2_POST=exposure=..,bloom=..,threshold=..,contrast=..,saturation=..,
grade=r/g/b,haze=..,inscatter=..,density=.. overrides the finishing pass
and haze (any subset; haze and inscatter multiply) for matching footage.
