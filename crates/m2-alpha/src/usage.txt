m2-alpha: Mercenaries 2 on a native Rust engine, from your own install.

m2-alpha [options]
  --install <dir>        the game's install folder (holding data\vz.wad); the EA app
                         folder in Program Files is found without it
  --spawn <x>,<z>        where on the island to start (default 450,-1950)
  --fly                  start with the free camera instead of on foot
  --model <hash>         a model to show (hex name hash; repeatable)
  --clip <hash>[@<s>]    with --model: a clip for each model to play (hex
                         hash; repeatable, in model order); with @seconds,
                         held still that far in
  --turn <degrees>       with --model: turn the models (90 shows a side)
  --gait <gait>          idle, walk, run or sprint: what characters shown
                         with --model play first; with --screenshot on the
                         island, how the player moves before the picture
  --config <file>        settings file (default m2-alpha.toml)
  --size <w>x<h>         window size
  --vsync on|off
  --fullscreen
  --sea <metres>         sea level (default -36, the game's)
  --double-sided         start with back-face culling off (as F3)
  --screenshot <out.ppm> render one frame offscreen and exit
  --drive                with --screenshot on the island: get in the
                         nearest parked vehicle and drive it forward first
                         (backing out first if it is boxed in, steering
                         round what it meets)
  --jump                 with --screenshot on the island: jump just before
                         the picture
  --throw                with --screenshot on the island: throw a grenade
                         just before the picture
  --lead <s>             with --jump or --throw: seconds before the picture
                         she starts (default 0.2)
  --fight                with --screenshot on the island: shoot the nearest
                         soldier, rocket a vehicle, throw a grenade first,
                         then look at the body
  --fight-wait <s>       with --fight or --blast: wait this long before
                         the picture
  --blast                with --screenshot on the island: fire a rocket at
                         the fuel tank or barrel nearest where she starts
  --vehicle <name>       with --drive: get in the nearest vehicle whose
                         name holds this (stryker, m1a1, amx30, hmmwv...)
  --look <degrees>       with --drive: stop and swing the camera this far
                         before the picture (turrets follow it)
  --spawns <n>           with --screenshot: list the n spawns nearest where
                         she starts (name, place, who they spawn)
  --face <degrees>       with --screenshot on foot: face this way first
                         (0 is +z, 90 is +x)
  --aim                  with --screenshot on foot: weapon up for the picture
  --weapon rifle|rpg     with --screenshot on foot: the weapon she has up
  --closeup              with --screenshot on foot: the picture from close by
                         her right side, to see her hands
  --probe                with --screenshot: list what rays from where she
                         stands hit (unseen walls, steps in the ground)
  --wreck                with --drive: wreck the vehicle at speed at the
                         end and log how it comes to rest
  --fire                 with --aim or --drive: fire just before the
                         picture
  --grip attach|hands    draw weapons on the hand's attach node as posed
                         (attach, the default), or held as worked out from
                         the hands (hands)
  --mission              run the game's mission scripts: the first contract
                         starts as in the game, with its objectives and
                         markers on screen (log: m2-alpha-mission.log);
                         with --screenshot, plays ten seconds of it first
  --mission-driver <lua> Lua to run before the scripts start, to test them
                         (crates/m2-game/examples/drivers/vzacon001.lua
                         plays the first contract); repeatable
  --mission-seconds <s>  with --screenshot: play this long instead of ten
  --mission-look <m>     with --mission --screenshot: take the picture from
                         this far from the nearest marker, looking at it
  --autoplay             with --mission --screenshot: she plays the contract
                         herself by its markers (runs to places, shoots the
                         marked soldiers, rockets the marked gates, takes
                         the vehicles brought, going a way planned round
                         walls, the sea and steep ground), logging how it
                         goes, until it is done or --mission-seconds are up;
                         each delivery is pictured beside the screenshot as
                         its helicopter hovers (<out>-delivery1.ppm, ...)
                         and once its load is down (-delivery1-down.ppm),
                         and the start's boat five seconds after she is
                         seated in it (-boat.ppm) and her three seconds
                         after she gets out of it (-ashore.ppm)
  --write-config         print the settings file and exit

On foot: WASD runs, Shift sprints, hold Q to walk, Space jumps.
E gets in or out of a vehicle (a row is parked ahead of where you start).
Driving: W/S throttle and reverse, A/D steer, Space brakes, R rights it.
Fighting (mouse captured): left button or F fires, right button aims,
1 rifle, 2 rocket launcher, G throws a grenade.
V switches to the free camera: WASD moves, Space/C up/down, Shift faster.
M music on/off, Tab or right mouse captures the mouse, N next gait
(--model), F11 fullscreen, F3 culling, F5 quick save, F9 quick load,
Escape opens the menu (settings, save and load, quit).
